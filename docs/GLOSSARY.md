# Glossary and discoverability index

**RSOS:** Research representation layer for individual cue meanings, glyphs, metaphors, programs and optional tiers.

**RSIA:** Assumption and reliability oversight: consent, signal uncertainty, watchdog decisions, abstention.

**RSX:** Typed program operator chains for cue presentation, memory prompts, rehearsal and optional branching.

**RSSO:** Orchestration of sessions, calibration, schedules, feedback and longitudinal records.

**Cue / conditioned cue:** An observed stimulus or user-defined association. A symbolic label alone is not evidence that conditioning has occurred.

**Glyph:** A user-selected symbol or mnemonic image, not a universal neurophysiological stimulus.

**Multimodal:** Combination of visual, auditory, tactile, kinesthetic, imagery or other user-authorized channels.

**Swish/override:** Historical and research-inspired attention/imagery techniques; clinical effectiveness is not implied by software implementation.

**Future pacing:** Voluntary imagination or rehearsal of future task situations, not proof those behaviors will occur.

**Tier:** Optional program-defined interval/progression label. It has no mandatory range or automatic equivalence to psychological levels.

**Compositional transfer:** Use of calibrated components or family relations to select previously untested combinations.

**Assumption watchdog:** Evidence-based decision of whether inferred cue relationships are supported or whether to use safer fallback/abstention.

**Source genealogy:** Document/message ancestry verified from source records; not the psychological or neural origin of an emotion.

**Brier score:** Proper scoring metric for probabilistic predictions.

**Regret:** In synthetic tests, the difference between a selected action's generator-defined value and the simulated oracle's value.

**Safety contract:** Bounded software permissions and checks, not clinical safety certification.
