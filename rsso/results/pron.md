# Pronunciation feedback on simulated learners

Generated by `python -m rsso.pron --evaluate`. Drills are synthesised from a vowel and melody model; each learner speaks in another voice (pitch -3 to +6 semitones), with another vocal tract (formants x0.92-1.12) and at another speed (x0.85-1.2), which feedback must ignore. Built and tested in simulation; effect unproven in people (claim C14).

| Learner | Runs | Any feedback given | The right thing named | And the right syllable |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A correct learner (other voice, other speed) | 40 | 2.5% | – | – |
| The last syllable falls where the model rises, or the reverse | 40 | 90% | 90% | 90% |
| One vowel replaced | 40 | 100% | 100% | 100% |
| The shortest syllable held 2.2 times as long | 40 | 100% | 92.5% | 92.5% |

Camera (simulated mouth traces, 40 runs each): a wrong mouth shape on one vowel found on the right syllable in 100%; correct shapes passed in 100%.

Reading: on the correct learner, any feedback is a false alarm. The model's voice is synthetic; real voices, consonants and noise will lower these numbers, which is what the protocol kit measures.
