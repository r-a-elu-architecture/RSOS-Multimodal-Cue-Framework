Reading: when the motor's drive leaks into the recording, a comparison with rest reports a brain response that is not there; the sham condition removes that, but only when it is subtracted with phase: compared by power alone (log SNR, 95% interval above 0), a real response and the pickup at different phases partly cancel and most real responses are missed. Trials must therefore start at the motor's onset. A response at the rhythm would still not show that focus improves or that a theta state follows: the TRIAL design `rhythm_focus` tests focus with the 2-back test.
