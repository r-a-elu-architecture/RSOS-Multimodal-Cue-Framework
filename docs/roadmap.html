From the design records to the build

Across three years of RSOS design records, one principle never changed: change a central node, and the change spreads to everything connected to it. The records hold 69 items worth building and 101 claims worth testing. This page is the plan: every item in build order, and every claim with the test it becomes. Nothing is discarded, and nothing is treated as established.
