4 · Scientific boundary and next test

What is supported: retrospective prediction of lexical operator categories improved in one selected RSOS-enriched historical archive. What is not supported: independent external generalization, unique superiority over modern transformers, autonomous architectural evolution, neural efficacy, or clinical impact. The next benchmark needs untouched external conversations, near-duplicate filtering, matched contextual transformer baselines and preregistered conversation-level evaluation.
