## Detecting a value conflict in the body (claim C62)

A rehearsed value conflict lowers RMSSD by 18% on average; an ordinary situation by nothing. A reaction is counted at a drop of 10% or more.

| Measurement | Conflicts detected | Ordinary situations wrongly flagged |
|---|---|---|
| Chest strap (noise 4%) | 98.5% | 0.5% |
| Phone camera (noise 8%) | 81% | 10.5% |

Reading: each measure finds a real change when there is one and rarely calls one when there is not, given enough ratings. Eight weeks is often too short to see a rise of one point a month; six months is enough. With five contexts a walk, recall speed shows a tree's advantage sooner than accuracy does. Single rehearsals measured by camera are noisy: repeat each value conflict and compare it with ordinary situations before trusting it.
