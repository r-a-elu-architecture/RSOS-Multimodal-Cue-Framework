## New
- **The copilot and session tools, THERA and LINX extensions** (`spec/COPILOT.md`, `rsso/rsso/copilot.py`, `docs/js/copilot.js`, Therapist page; roadmap step 7, items 8, 12, 13, 14, 16, 17, 20, 40, 41, 47, 50 and 69): the decision matrix as an anchor tree with a copilot on the live transcript, agreement (Cohen's kappa) and response time; the level of a sentence and the five-step chain (detect, analyse, modify, output, future-pace) with unsafe lines held; typology plug-ins (sensory words, time orientation) with consent for profiling others; craving context triggers and cue-reactivity per month; Core Transformation one question at a time, to an RSX program; rescripting with the symbol and light; the life timeline with memory specificity; overrides, start latency, OXQ and monthly success, with DOL-9 as an exact 90-second RSX loop; perceptual events always routed to escalation; speech contexts, the four-day block and the timed burst; the setting of each session. Programs open in the player. Simulated sessions and people (`rsso/results/copilot.md`). Protocol kit (`spec/kits/COPILOT_PROTOCOL_KIT.md`). Python and JavaScript identical (tested).
- **SLEEP and REWARD** (`spec/NIGHT.md`, `rsso/rsso/night.py`, `docs/js/night.js`, `docs/night.html`; roadmap step 6, items 2, 3, 6, 18 and 28): the night plan with trauma-related codes held back and a safety code or gentle waking for nightmares; a closed-loop controller that cues only after 90 seconds of deep sleep as read by the device, below waking level, backs off after any arousal, pauses five minutes and stops at a nightly limit; a seeded simulated sleeper with sleep cycles, a device that misreads stages and cue-caused arousals; night reports against random cueing and no cueing; next-day recall of cued against uncued items with an interval; a night diary; reward codes per learning area with prefixes, rate limits and latency; recognising a good move from an accelerometer by its shape; the notice test for light with vibration. Simulated people with known answers (`rsso/results/night.md`). Protocol kit (`spec/kits/NIGHT_PROTOCOL_KIT.md`). Night in the site menu. Python and JavaScript identical (tested).
- **ATLAS, the trigger atlas and the transfer engine** (`spec/ATLAS.md`, `rsso/rsso/atlas.py`, `docs/js/atlas.js`, `docs/atlas.html`; roadmap step 5, items 5, 57–61, 63, 65 and 67): sampled moments across the senses with control moments; node and combination weights fitted together by least squares with 95% intervals and verdicts; readiness and what to sample next; exact leave-one-out prediction; rerouting a strong trigger to the strongest proven anchor through the autonomy gate; transfer to untreated triggers measured against a repeat mapping that removes habituation; ten higher nodes by abstraction and scope with a level sweep; the original glyphs walked in ledger order as a timed memory route. The page draws the network as a living 2D map and a live 3D guided descent (WebGL without a library, 2D fallback, reduced-motion cuts). TRIAL design `network_vs_timeline`. Simulated people with known triggers (`rsso/results/atlas.md`). Protocol kit (`spec/kits/ATLAS_PROTOCOL_KIT.md`). Atlas in the site menu. Python and JavaScript identical (tested).
- **IDENTITY, identity profiles level by level** (`spec/IDENTITY.md`, `rsso/rsso/identity.py`, `docs/js/identity.js`, `docs/identity.html`; roadmap step 4, items 5, 11, 23, 24, 25, 34, 35, 37, 39, 51 and 55): a profile headed by a symbol and login code over seven levels, each with statement, image and code (codes checked so the body can tell them apart), an environment layer, a language and a policy; versions with field-by-field differences; coherence (how true the levels feel, less their spread) and its monthly trend with an interval; drift after time with other people; generalisation to untrained places with time to start; anchor-tree recall walks against a single image; value-conflict rehearsals with RMSSD and recovery after the symbol; switching by login code, schedule and interrupt with time to settle. Programs: the symbol by day, the whole chain at night (flagged, needs consent), four steps, the prepared week and a month's dose, sent to the player. A library of six profiles. TRIAL design `identity_language`. Simulated people with known answers (`rsso/results/identity.md`). Protocol kit (`spec/kits/IDENTITY_PROTOCOL_KIT.md`). Identity in the site menu. Python and JavaScript identical (tested).
- **The autonomy gate** (`spec/GATE.md`, `rsso/rsso/gate.py`, `docs/js/gate.js`, Safety page; roadmap step 3, items 4, 26 and 56): each anchor sits at a stage (manual, suggest, automatic, under pressure) earned from PEAK captures and strength, confirmed offers with few false alarms, and no wear-out or contamination; the gate is computed at every decision and closes by itself when evidence weakens. Wear-out counts uses since the last pairing (warn at 15, no automatic use from 25). Consent records per scope, flags on actions below awareness, and an audit log of automatic decisions. The player offers or plays the best anchor when stress is detected, as the gate allows; the Anchors page shows each anchor's stage. Simulated people over 60 days (`rsso/results/gate.md`): strong anchors automatic around day 11, no-effect anchors never fire by themselves, contaminated anchors lose automatic use within a day in 75% of people. Protocol kit (`spec/kits/GATE_PROTOCOL_KIT.md`). Python and JavaScript identical (tested).
- **TRIAL and CHECKS, personal trials and short tasks** (`spec/TRIAL.md`, `spec/CHECKS.md`, `rsso/rsso/trial.py`, `docs/js/trial.js`, `rsso/rsso/checks.py`, `docs/js/checks.js`, `docs/trials.html`; roadmap step 2): five ready-made trial designs (anchor or sham, cue or none, automatic or by hand, four orders of Core Transformation and identity, the three SSIT arms), permuted-block schedules from a seed, sessions with outcome before and after and background factors (sleep, caffeine, exercise, hour); each arm against the reference with a Welch 95% interval, a permutation p-value, an estimate adjusted for session order and sleep, a balance table and a verdict; random-effects pooling across people; a consistency measure for repeated decisions. Six tasks with seeded sequences and standard scoring: 2-back (d′), digit span, reaction time calm and under pressure, word recall, reading speed and comprehension (original passages), and dot-probe attention to threat. Simulated people with known effects (`rsso/results/trial.md`): a personal effect of 1.5 points found in 63.5% of people with 12 blocks, false claims under 5%, 30 people pooled to 1.14 [0.74, 1.54] for a true 1.0. Protocol kit for series of N-of-1 trials (`spec/kits/TRIAL_PROTOCOL_KIT.md`). Trials in the site menu; the menu now sits on its own row under the name. Python and JavaScript identical (tested).
- **PEAK, anchors as measured objects** (`spec/PEAK.md`, `rsso/rsso/peak.py`, `docs/js/peak.js`, `rsso/rsso/ppg.py`, `docs/js/ppg.js`, `docs/anchors.html`; roadmap step 1): an anchor holds its channels (vibration code, scent, light, phrase, glyph), its role (healing, stability, redirection, lock-in), the body signature of each capture, its probes and its uses. Capture at a peak by self-tap, an HRV rise or EEG; blind probe trials, one in three a sham code; strength against sham with a 95% interval and a status (building, strong, present, weakening, contaminated, unclear); contamination detection and a restoration plan; two-symbol anchors (🦁 + 🐉, `..- ---`); chains in role order with a per-phase report, and each chain as an RSX program. Heart rate and RMSSD from a fingertip over the phone camera, with a quality score. A seeded simulated body with known anchor strengths: with 8 cue and 4 sham probes, strong anchors found in 100% of simulated people, no-effect anchors called an effect in 0%, contaminated anchors flagged in 100% with 0.7% false flags (`rsso/results/peak.md`). Protocol kit for real studies (`spec/kits/PEAK_PROTOCOL_KIT.md`). Anchors in the site menu. Python and JavaScript identical (tested).

- **Metric registry and operational metrics** (`rsso/rsso/metrics.py`, `docs/js/metrics.js`, `docs/metrics.html`): 54 codes with every name, definition and recorded value; 20 computed for each person from the run log (Python and JavaScript identical), 12 measured by the system's tests, the rest kept as records.
- **Nodes** (`spec/NODES.md`, `rsso/rsso/nodes.py`, `docs/js/nodes.js`, node studio `docs/nodes.html`, player 1.9.0): a node is a desired state held as one unit — cues on every sense (V A K O G), an original glyph, a vibration code (Lion `..-`, Raven `.-.`, Leopard `...`, Observer `-` …), a mode (associated, or observer: nothing binds, only observed) and the mental frame said in that state. The node studio takes symptoms, triggers, heart-rate parameters, devices and desired states, and builds the plan: nodes with a frame check (scope, process, negation, believability), an RSX program per node, the ledger tiers and metrics, the dose (50 a day for 4 days, then spaced), devices and safety (therapist-led observer work for trauma, screening, allergy check). It runs in the player only after consent, and can be copied as a prompt for an AI assistant (LINX). RSX gains `FRAME`, `MODE` and `TACTON` tier fields; the compiler plays the vibration code on activation. Python and JavaScript identical (tested).
- **Glyphs drawn as written, each with its own vibration code** (player 1.10.0): every page now draws the original glyphs as text in the same fonts as the ledger pages, sharp at any size (the Lion on the player's light included); the stored images remain the reference for the shape rhythm and melody, and the fallback where a device has no font. Tier 013 is kept as the ledger writes it, 🦁 + 🐉, as its own glyph (C001). Every glyph has a unique vibration code in short and long pulses (Lion `..-`, Dragon `---`, observer ∅ `-`; when the single codes run out, codes are recombined with a pause; 🦁 + 🐉 is `..- ---`); "Feel it" plays it on the wrist and as the sound of a vibration, with the dots and dashes lighting in time (spec/GLYPH_FORMAT.md §7). The ledger order view shows every glyph a tier records. RSX `TACTON` accepts recombined codes. The player shows the site navigation.
- **Research data contribution** (`spec/DATA_CONTRIBUTION.md`, `rsso/rsso/contribution.py`, `docs/js/contribution.js`, `docs/contribute.html`, player 1.11.0): one de-identified, checksummed file per person, clinician or lab, with twelve consent scopes (runs required; physiology, plan, own words, calibration, code check, questionnaires, safety, profile, health, clinical, lab chosen). Dates become day numbers, ids are random, own words and health data only when shared; clinician files need the client's consent, lab files an ethics reference. Clinicians add sessions with fidelity and CGI ratings; labs describe devices and fingerprint recordings, and download BIDS `events.tsv` for any program to align EEG with every cue. A one-minute vibration-code recognition check. `python -m rsso.contribution check|pool` verifies files and pools all runs into one table for MEMSTACK, the metrics and the analysis kit. The player now records each run's full context (program fingerprint, outputs, breath, glyph, code, node mode, duration, hour of day, heart rate before and after) and links to the page. Python and JavaScript build identical files (tested).
- **LINX, the AI layer** (`spec/LINX.md`, `rsso/rsso/linx.py`, `docs/js/linx.js`, player 1.12.0): the three operators the archive recovers, built. A conversation in the node studio reads plain language ("stress at work when my boss shouts; I want calm confidence, like a lion, with the smell of cedar") and fills the plan, asking one question at a time for what is missing. Tier-state injection: a prompt for any language model with the person's nodes, ledger tiers, practice summary and a reply schema. The containment router checks a model's proposal field by field (nothing may start a run, frames must pass the frame check, unsafe wording is held back for the person and their therapist) and applies only what passes. Node offers: when the heart rate signals stress, the player offers the node that fits best, with its reasons, within the safety limits. Frame suggestions that score higher, one click to use. Python and JavaScript identical (tested).
- **CONTAIN, the safety layer** (`spec/CONTAIN.md`, `rsso/rsso/contain.py`, `docs/js/contain.js`, `docs/safety.html`, player 1.13.0): the policy engine the archive names but does not recover. A device safety envelope no program can exceed; drift detection that pauses a program or node when distress after it rises or agency falls (and at once when an adverse event is linked to it); escalation that pauses device offers and therapist-led work and points to a therapist or crisis help; one decide() gate asked by the player, the heart-rate offers, the node studio and the Python runtime, with every decision logged with its reason; restore by the person, or by the therapist when harm was involved; rollback to the previous node plan. The player gains a Safety panel ("Something felt wrong"); reported events flow into research contributions. Conformance C14 and C15: 15/15 on the virtual rig and the firmware core. Python and JavaScript identical (tested).
- **THERA, therapist mode** (`spec/THERA.md`, `rsso/rsso/thera.py`, `docs/js/thera.js`, `docs/data/thera.json`, `docs/therapist.html`): structured, measured sessions a therapist runs with the client's own nodes, each step tagged with the archive's operator (IdentityRebuild, BreathOverride, EmotionalLock, VIB-ANCHOR, FP-ANCHOR, EthicsContain). Five protocols (stress, boundaries, habits, low mood, therapist-led observer work with a memory). What to say, do and measure at each step, with a link that runs the step's node or program in the player; stop rules (screening, a distress ceiling, adverse events); a distress line through the session; the summary with fidelity, distress and agency, runs and CGI ratings, which becomes a research-contribution session; the practice plan between sessions. Every session start is checked by CONTAIN. Python and JavaScript identical (tested).
- **CM1, the compression layer** (`spec/CM1.md`, `rsso/rsso/cm1.py`, `docs/js/cm1.js`, `docs/data/cm1.json`): each person's practice compressed into a 13-number signature; response profiles fitted across people and described in plain words; a new person assigned from a few runs with where to start (kind of node, which sense carries the strongest cue), shown on the metrics page and in the node studio; patterns that repeat across everyone with 95% intervals; a device bundle of a person's nodes, codes, glyphs, dose and safety state in about 40 bytes. On a synthetic population with known profiles, the right profile from the first 10 runs for 100% (clear) and 96% (hard setting) of held-out people (`rsso/results/cm1.md`). Python and JavaScript identical (tested).
- **Phones**: the Devices page starts with what works on Android and iPhone (Android is a complete RSOS device; iPhone browsers block vibration and Bluetooth) and an eight-step Android set-up; the player links to it.
- **Board kit and wristband page** (`firmware/BOARD_KIT.md`, `rsso/rsso/board.py`, `docs/js/rdp.js`, `docs/device.html`): parts, wiring and flashing for ESP32, -C3 and -S3 (Arduino IDE, arduino-cli, PlatformIO; the sketch compile-checked for Arduino-ESP32 2.x and 3.x, pins set per board, and capped in hardware at the safety envelope). The board check B1–B7 (HELLO, own-clock timing, intensity clamp, Stop within 100 ms, consent, release cap, a glyph's vibration code) runs on a real band over USB (`python -m rsso.board --port`), with the whole conformance suite in real time (`--full`); the same check runs in the browser over USB or Bluetooth on the new Wristband page, which also plays the vibration codes of nodes and programs on the band after CONTAIN's check. 7/7 against the firmware core in real time; not yet run on a real board. Which wristbands work is stated on the page and in the kit: self-built and flashable ESP32 bands, RDP/1 devices and Android phones' vibration yes; closed brand watches and Wear OS or Apple Watch not until RSOS watch apps exist.
- **Review package** (`REVIEW.md`, `review/REPORT_TEMPLATE.md`, `scripts/check_all.sh`, `requirements.txt`, `.github/workflows/checks.yml`): reproduce every result with one command, also on every push; an evidence register marking every number as measured, simulated or synthetic; eleven open findings from an internal pre-review (which wristbands RSOS can run on, Bluetooth pairing, encryption at rest, re-identification in small samples, clinical validation, regulatory status, first board, and more); a checklist per area.
