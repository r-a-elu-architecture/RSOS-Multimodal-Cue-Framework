The glyphs of RSOS

Every glyph the tier ledger records, exactly as written, from the lion of Tier 001 to the mutation chain of the later epochs. Each one also has a vibration code of its own, in short and long pulses, and a melody derived from its shape, so a cue can move between the senses. Select a glyph to hear it; “Feel it” plays its code on the wrist and as the sound of a vibration.

The generated library (optional)

An extra set of 48 glyphs drawn from strokes, evolved by mutation and chosen to be as different from each other as possible. They are not from the ledger.

Glyph data: the original glyphs, originals.json (built from the tier ledger by rsso/rsso/ugcl_originals.py); the generated library, library.json, and its evolved population, genealogy.json. Vibration works on phones that support it (most Android browsers).
