Every tier, as it was recorded

The tier ledger is the development record of RSOS: each tier is a function, a glyph, a command, and how the system behaved when it first appeared. Nothing here is edited or left out. Each tier also shows where it now lives in the architecture and, where a tier works below awareness, what it means and the consent it needs.
