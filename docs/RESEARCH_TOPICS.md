# Research topics and technical questions

## Compositional transfer and adaptive personalization

Can a controller infer useful new glyph–sound–context programs from limited person-specific calibration? When does grouped cue-family structure generalize, and when should the system fall back to individually tested cues? Benchmark includes random, fixed, additive, all-pairs, evidence-gated and equally informed generic model-selection policies. The published numerical differences are **synthetic**.

See [v1.3 draft](../research/compositional-transfer-v1.3/PREPRINT_DRAFT.md).

## EEG, BCI and physiological inputs

The acquisition layer may accept EEG, HRV or other calibrated features with quality/confidence metadata. EEG band-power alone does **not** identify a specific emotion, belief, trauma memory or cue meaning. An offline adapter is an interface proposal, not demonstrated neurofeedback treatment. Required: artifact detection, participant consent, independently specified labels, held-out subject tests, and robust abstention.

## Cue-response learning, counterconditioning and rehearsal

Research hypotheses include learned cue associations, mnemonic imagery, voluntary intervention choices, future-context rehearsal, swish-inspired attentional redirects, and sensory pairing. These are **candidate operators**; their effects must be isolated experimentally with genuine human outcomes and dose-matched controls. The framework cannot infer the installation of a new identity or mental model from program execution.

## Mnemonics, VR and contextual assistance

A user can map a personal glyph to a studied concept, chosen communication principle, memory cue, or task context. A context classifier can offer a suggestion only after explicit opt-in, when observation quality is sufficient, and must tolerate alternative user-defined meanings.

## Provenance, authorization and auditability

Document-lineage proofs and scoped authorization bind a request to verified history and permission. They do not establish the neurological cause of a response. See [proof-carrying systems research](../research/privacy-preserving-adaptation-v0.8/PAPER_ABSTRACT.md).

## Evaluation

For each claim report underlying data type, task, strongest equally informed baseline, uncertainty, failure cases, independent test, and safety boundaries. In particular, generic controllers with the same relationships and gating rules have matched the RSOS-inspired software.
