# Frequently asked questions

### Is RSOS a treatment or medical device?

No. The current public release describes a programmable research framework and simulation/software methods. Trauma treatment, clinical diagnosis and therapeutic efficacy have not been established.

### Does an EEG headset reveal emotion, trauma or a particular belief?

Not from alpha/beta band-power alone. Valid task-specific decoding requires independently observed labels, artifact controls, held-out subjects and external validation. Physiological signals should be treated as uncertain inputs.

### Can the same glyph mean different things to different people?

Yes. Meanings are scoped to a person, goal and context. A public "Lion" label does not imply a universal state or measurable neural effect.

### Must users progress through tiers 1–605?

No. Tier ranges are optional, program-specific annotations, not physiological milestones.

### Are the improvements measured in real people?

The highlighted v1.3 decision-regret result is from synthetic environments. Other project tracks include real historical *document* lineage, not verified treatment response. Null findings and generic-controller ties are published.

### Is the system a new invention with no prior art?

No such determination has been established. The components overlap established work in JITAIs, adaptive learning, cue conditioning, runtime assurance, mnemonic methods and provenance. The research asks whether an integrated system adds measurable value.

### Is everything in the historical copyright archives open-source?

Not automatically. A historical specification may be publicly readable while remaining under different legal terms from executable software. See [rights](SOURCES_AND_RIGHTS.md).

### How can a lab collaborate?

Reproduce a benchmark, improve baselines, submit a privacy audit, or design a neutral mnemonic-learning study with informed consent and independent oversight. See [research topics](RESEARCH_TOPICS.md).
