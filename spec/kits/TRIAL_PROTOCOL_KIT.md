# TRIAL protocol kit: series of personal randomised trials

A ready protocol for running RSOS personal trials with real people: each participant runs their own randomised trial, and the trials pool into a group result. Module specifications: [TRIAL.md](../TRIAL.md), [CHECKS.md](../CHECKS.md).

## 1. Question

For a chosen design (for example `anchor_vs_sham` or `ssit`), does the experimental arm change the outcome more than the reference arm, for each participant and on average across participants, and how much do participants differ?

## 2. Design

- **A series of N-of-1 trials** (reported following the CONSORT extension for N-of-1 trials).
- **Permuted blocks**, 8 to 12 blocks per participant, order generated by the page from a recorded seed.
- **Blinding** where the design allows it (anchor against sham: the participant is not told which code played).
- **Washout**: one session a day at most, at least 6 hours apart, so one session does not carry into the next.
- **Sample size**: the simulation finds a personal effect of 1.5 points in about two thirds of people with 12 blocks; a group of 30 people with 8 blocks each estimates an average effect of 1.0 point to within about ±0.4. Plan from your own pilot's spread.

## 3. Measures

| Measure | When |
|---|---|
| The design's outcome (calm 0-10, or ease of an untrained activity 0-10) | Before and after each session |
| Background factors: hours slept, caffeine, exercise, hour of day | Each session |
| CHECKS tasks (for example reading, or n-back) | As the outcome in SSIT designs, and at baseline and the end in all designs |
| Adverse events | Any time (the Safety page) |

## 4. Consent wording (adapt to your ethics board)

> You will compare two or more ways of practising, one session a day. The website decides at random which way each session uses, so that the comparison is fair. You will rate how you feel before and after each session, and sometimes do short memory or reading tasks. Your results are stored without your name. You can stop at any time. The methods are experimental and are not a treatment.

## 5. Data format

One file per participant: `{"schema": "rsos-trial/1", "design", "blocks", "seed", "schedule", "sessions": [{"i", "arm", "ts", "before", "after", "cov"}]}`, as the page exports it. Dates become day numbers before sharing; the research contribution format (spec/DATA_CONTRIBUTION.md) can carry these files.

## 6. Analysis

```
python -m rsso.trial analyze participant.json
```

prints each contrast with its interval, permutation p-value and verdict. For the group, pool the per-participant contrasts with `rsso.trial.pool` (random effects), or fit a mixed model with participant as a random effect and arm, session order and sleep as fixed effects.

## 7. Report template

1. Participants: number, recruitment, exclusions, completed blocks.
2. Design, arms, seed and schedule generation; blinding and how it was checked.
3. Per-participant effects with intervals (a forest plot), and the verdict for each.
4. Pooled effect with its interval, and the spread between participants.
5. Balance of background factors across arms; adjusted estimates.
6. CHECKS results at baseline and end.
7. Adverse events.
8. What this means for the claims tested (see the design table in TRIAL.md).
