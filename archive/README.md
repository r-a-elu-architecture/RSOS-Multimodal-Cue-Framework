# RSOS / RSIA / RSX / RSSO — historical source archive

This directory preserves the **original dated source records** supplied for public disclosure. These are primary historical documents: descriptions and original claims **are not proof** that an underlying model, physiological effect, neural state, or claimed intellectual-property entitlement was independently established.

## Navigating the archive

- [`original-package/`](original-package/) — intact, unmodified source ZIP.
- [`original-estates/`](original-estates/) — all 65 original UTF-8 files at their original paths, including the nine duplicated contents occurring at distinct historical paths. 15 of them are a public edition with targeted wording edits (see below).
- [`provenance/SOURCE-MANIFEST.csv`](provenance/SOURCE-MANIFEST.csv) — hashes, original paths, byte counts, source categories.
- [`provenance/DUPLICATE-MAP.csv`](provenance/DUPLICATE-MAP.csv) — nine exact duplicate file-content groups, **not** deletion instructions.
- [`provenance/SHA256SUMS.txt`](provenance/SHA256SUMS.txt) — independently checkable SHA-256s for ZIP and all files.
- [`../architecture/`](../architecture/) — modern, source-linked navigation and interpretations, separate from immutable originals.

## Archive integrity and interpretation

Files have not been sanitized, rewritten or deduplicated. Fifteen files carry targeted public-edition edits only: the Fox archetype was renamed Raven with non-manipulative wording, the *48 Laws of Power* mnemonic references were replaced with Stephen Covey's *7 Habits of Highly Effective People*, and RSSO-versus-Tribit comparison passages were removed. Line counts are unchanged and all other text is untouched. The original bytes remain in `original-package/` and their hashes are listed beside the edited hashes in [`provenance/PUBLIC-EDITION-EDITS.csv`](provenance/PUBLIC-EDITION-EDITS.csv). All 65 original internal paths are preserved within `original-estates/`; the source ZIP retains its original internal directory layout. Texts labeled `PASS31B` include later historical reconstructions and evidence excerpts; do not assign their assertions the same provenance as original specifications. Some original texts contain proprietary declarations, author contact details, psychological terminology and historical assistant claims. These reflect their authorship and source context, not verified extraordinary model capabilities.
