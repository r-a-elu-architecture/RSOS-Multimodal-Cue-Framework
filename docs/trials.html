Find out what works for you

Compare two or more ways of practising. The page decides at random which one each session uses, so your mood and expectations cannot tilt the result. After enough sessions you get your own answer, with an interval: this works better for you, the other does, or there is no clear difference yet.

Built and tested in simulationEffect unproven in people

Your trial

Short tasks

One to three minutes each. Your scores are kept with the date so you can compare yourself over weeks. They are research measures, not diagnoses, and they improve a little with practice at first.

See it on simulated people

Thirty simulated people whose real effect of an anchor over a sham varies around one point. Each runs eight blocks. Every line is one person's result with its interval; the diamond is all of them pooled.

Across many simulated people: rsso/results/trial.md. To run trials with real people: the protocol kit.
