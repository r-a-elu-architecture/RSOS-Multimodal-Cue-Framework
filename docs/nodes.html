Design the states you want to live in

A node is a state held as one unit: a sphere of light, the sound, the feeling, the scent, the taste, a vibration on the wrist, and the words you say in that state. Practised together, any one of them opens the whole state, including the way you see.

Describe what is happening and the states you want. The studio builds the plan: nodes, cues, codes, glyphs, frames, the ledger tiers and metrics to use, and how often to practise. You, or you with your therapist, approve it; the player runs it.

Describe it in your own words

LINX, the AI layer of RSOS, reads what you write, asks one question at a time for what is missing, and fills in the plan below. You can still change everything by hand. It runs in this browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

1What is happening

What do you, or the person you work with, want to change? Choose what applies and describe it in your own words.

2The body and the devices

Resting heart rate and variability let the heart-rate trigger learn your baseline. Leave them empty if you don't know them.

3Your nodes

Which states do you want, and which symbol holds each one: a lion for courage and boundaries, a raven for the wide view, the observer for no ego, where nothing binds? Start from a symbol or make your own.

4How much practice

A node becomes a reflex through repetition. The original override was practised by interrupting 50 negative states a day; a shorter, spaced plan works too.

5Approve

Read every node on the right. A plan runs only with the consent of the person it is for.
