Programmed cues

Install a thought, a chain of thoughts, a metaphor or a behaviour you choose, so it becomes automatic. Write it yourself, or with your therapist during a session. Every cue needs your consent and stays listed here; you can revoke any of them at any time.

On your phone

Android (Chrome): everything works, including vibration codes on the phone, a heart-rate strap and an RSOS wristband over Bluetooth. iPhone: light, words, voice and tones work; vibration and Bluetooth do not, because iPhone browsers block them.

Set up on Android in a few steps, and see what works on each phone.

Heart-rate trigger

Connect a Bluetooth heart-rate strap or watch that broadcasts heart rate. When your heart rate rises and its variability drops for several seconds, the player offers the override. Works in Chrome or Edge on Android, Windows, macOS and Linux.
