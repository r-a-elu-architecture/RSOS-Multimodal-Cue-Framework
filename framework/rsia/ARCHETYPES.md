## What an archetype is

An RSIA archetype is an **internal mental model**: a mode of perception, language and bodily state that a person runs on their *own* cognition to shape their own state. Archetypes are not protocols for acting on other people.

Every archetype:
- operates on the **Spine** (Bushidō virtues and principled frameworks), which no archetype may override;
- is selected by the **Watchdog** according to context and internal state;
- is overseen by the **Meta-Architect**, which sets long-term direction and can lock, combine or retire archetypes;
- can carry a **mnemonic set** (see `../mnemonic_sets/`) whose items are encoded as glyphs.

## Core archetypes

**1. Lion — Courage & Authority.**
Strength, agency and calm command. The anchor archetype of the Override Protocol (lion light).
Mnemonics: presence and leadership frameworks.
Primary use: leadership, public speaking, recovering agency after a negative state.

**2. Eagle — Vision & Strategy.**
Foresight, perception and high-level overview.
Mnemonics: strategic foresight, planning frameworks.
Primary use: long-term planning, decisions under uncertainty.

**3. Bear — Resilience & Grounding.**
Stability, endurance and grounding.
Mnemonics: endurance principles, grounding routines.
Primary use: stability during crisis, carrying identity through adversity.

**4. Leopard — Agility & Precision.**
Speed, adaptive response and precise action.
Mnemonics: rapid-learning and adaptive frameworks.
Primary use: fast shifts between tasks and contexts.

**5. Raven — Insight & Strategic Perception.**
Intelligence, insight, foresight and principled judgment.
Mnemonics: Stephen Covey's *7 Habits of Highly Effective People* (user-loaded, see `../mnemonic_sets/`), principled negotiation, recognition of manipulation patterns.
Primary use: strategic perception, clear negotiation, recognising manipulation, and internal reframing that pre-empts negative states.

**6. Tiger — Intensity & Drive.**
Full commitment, discipline and sustained effort.
Mnemonics: discipline and perseverance frameworks.
Primary use: pushing through resistance in one's own performance and habits.

## Roles in one line each

Lion leads. Eagle plans. Bear sustains. Leopard adapts. Raven perceives. Tiger drives.
