# CM1 on a synthetic population with known profiles

160 virtual people from 4 true profiles (sound-led, scent-led, observer-helped, struggling), 30 runs each. CM1 fits 4 profiles on half of them; the other 80 are assigned from only their first 10 runs. The hard setting puts the profiles twice as close together, makes the fastest sense less consistent and the ratings noisier.

| | Clear profiles | Hard setting |
|---|---|---|
| Right profile from the first 10 runs | 80/80 (100%) | 77/80 (96%) |
| Right profile from all 30 runs | 80/80 (100%) | 80/80 (100%) |
| Suggests the kind of program that truly helps most, from the first 10 runs (people whose profile has a clear best) | 60/60 (100%) | 58/60 (97%) |

Profiles found (clear setting):

|---|---|---|---|
