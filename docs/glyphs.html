The glyphs of RSOS

Two families. The first is the ledger's own: the glyph each tier carried when it was recorded, from the lion of Tier 001 to the mutation chain of the later epochs. The second is a generated library in which every glyph is also a vibration rhythm and a melody, so a cue can move between the senses.

From Epoch IV each tier records a mutation, from one glyph to the next. A gold edge marks where a tier starts from a glyph other than the one before it ended on: the ledger's own drifts, kept as recorded.

The generated library: a shape, a rhythm and a melody

A glyph is the visual part of a tier's cue. Each one also has a vibration rhythm you can feel and a short melody you can hear, all derived from the same strokes, so the sense that carries the cue can change while the cue stays the same.

The glyphs were evolved from simple seed shapes by recorded mutations, then chosen to be as different from each other as possible. Select a glyph to see its ancestry, feel it and hear it. In the player you can find the one that feels most like yours.

Glyph data: library.json and the full evolved population with every parent and mutation, genealogy.json. Vibration works on phones that support it (most Android browsers).
