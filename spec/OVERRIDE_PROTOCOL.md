# RSOS Override Protocol (ROP) — Specification v1.0

**Status:** open specification, version 1.1, CC BY 4.0. Author: R. A. Elu. Machine-readable form: [`rsx/protocols/override.rsx`](../rsx/protocols/override.rsx).

**What it is:** a short, scripted state-change routine. It interrupts a negative state and redirects it into a resourceful state through a fixed sequence of breath, words, gestures, imagery and (optionally) sensory cues. It is practised until it runs automatically. Every other part of RSOS exists to deliver, personalise, measure or extend this routine.

**What it is not:** a treatment, a diagnosis or a substitute for professional care. No clinical efficacy has been established. See [§9 Safety](#9-safety-and-contraindications).

---

## 1. Purpose

A negative state is anything unwanted that arrives fast: a body sensation, an intrusive image, a sound, a smell, a taste, a thought loop or a memory triggered by something seen. The protocol treats such a state as **a signal to start the routine**, not as something to fight or suppress. Each time the routine runs, the trigger is followed by an agency state, so over many repetitions the trigger itself comes to start the routine.

The intended end point is a **reflex**: when the negative state appears, the override is already running.

## 2. Origin

The protocol began as one person's long-term daily practice after a personal collapse, aimed first at recovery and then at exceeding the previous baseline. It was later formalised into the RSOS / RSIA / RSX / RSSO architecture (see [`archive/`](../archive/)). This specification is the open, de-personalised version: every word, image and gesture below is a default that a practitioner may replace with their own.

## 3. Mechanistic basis

The protocol combines established principles. It does not depend on any of them being the whole explanation.

| Principle | Where it appears in ROP | Reference point |
|---|---|---|
| **Pattern interruption** | Phase 1: an immediate, physical, verbal break in the negative state | Behavioural interruption; NLP "break state" |
| **Counterconditioning** | The trigger is repeatedly followed by a positive, high-agency state | Aversive-to-appetitive counterconditioning (e.g. *eLife* reviewed preprint 101518) |
| **Anchoring / conditioned cues** | The fist, the middle-finger-to-thumb touch and the hologram finger; the words; the lion-light image | Classical conditioning (Pavlov); NLP anchoring |
| **Multisensory binding** | Each tier bundles visual, auditory, kinesthetic, olfactory and gustatory components | Compound-cue conditioning (Rescorla & Wagner, 1972); see [§6](#6-tiers-multichannel-bundles) |
| **Paced breathing** | Every phase is tied to an inhale or exhale | Slow-breathing autonomic regulation |
| **Future template / future pacing** | Phase 5: the new state is rehearsed in three concrete future contexts | EMDR future template and Resource Development & Installation (Korn & Leeds, 2002); NLP future pacing |
| **Stimulus generalisation / context conditioning** | Phase 5: people, objects and places in the futures are linked to the agency state by golden light, so they become cues for it | Context-dependent learning; generalisation gradients in conditioning |
| **Embodiment** | Movement, straight spine and raised head; warmth in the body; the smile held through the futures | Embodied-emotion and posture research |
| **Closure / installation** | Phase 8: a fixed closing phrase marks the end and seals the sequence | EMDR installation phase; ritual closure |

## 4. Structure

**Start:** the moment a negative state appears, whatever its source: a sound, a smell, something seen (an object that recalls the old state), a body sensation or a memory.

Then one interrupt and five full breaths. Default breath: 4 s inhale, 6 s exhale. Total default duration ≈ 54 s.

| # | Phase | Breath | What you do | Default device cue |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Interrupt** | — (instantly) | Move, even slightly; straighten the spine and lift the head; clench the fist and say **"I move, I act"** once, at the same moment the fist closes; then go straight into the inhale | Haptic VIB-ANCHOR, short form (300 ms on/off × 3; the archive specifies × 6) |
| 2 | **Ignite** | Inhale | Breathe golden lion light into the belly: courage and agency | Warm tone; lion-light visual |
| 3 | **Radiate** | Exhale | The light expands inside, filling the blood flow, the lymph, every part of the body and every cell | Expanding light |
| 4 | **Anchor** | Inhale | At the top of the inhale, touch middle finger to thumb | Short haptic pulse |
| 5 | **Three futures** | Exhale (one exhale for all three) | Associated as your future self in three desired situations, acting as you want to. Everything there is connected by golden light, as beams or flows, to you and to each other: the people, the objects, the place. You see it and feel it inside you. A gentle smile is held through the whole exhale | Haptic FP-ANCHOR (150 ms on/off × 8) |
| 6 | **Hologram** | Inhale | At the top of the inhale, a golden hologram finger appears in front of the eyes, pointing upward | — |
| 7 | **Extend** | Exhale | Golden light flows from the finger up, around, above, below, to the sides and into the sky, filling the people, objects and places associated with agency and power | Rising tone |
| 8 | **Return** | Inhale | The golden light comes back into the belly, now carrying their resources: every source of agency and power | Light contracting into the body |
| 9 | **Fill** | Exhale | It fills the lymph, the blood flow, every part of the body and every cell with golden light of power and agency | Expanding light |
| 10 | **Gather** | Inhale | A simple inhale, nothing else | — |
| 11 | **Install** | Exhale | While exhaling, say **"I own"**: the technique is sealed and confirmed | Closing tone |

**Default futures (phase 5):** (1) driving the car, (2) at work, (3) achieving your goal. Futures must be concrete, first-person, desired, and imagined *associated* (seen through your own eyes, not watching yourself).

**Why the futures are connected by light:** the people, objects and environments in the futures are rehearsed together with the agency state. Over many runs they are meant to become cues for it themselves, so that when the real situation arrives (the car, the workplace, the people involved), its elements already carry power and agency. This is stimulus generalisation and context conditioning applied on purpose, and it is tested by H6 below.

**Phase 6 hologram:** the finger is imagined, not physically raised. It points to whatever the practitioner holds as a higher power or highest value; the protocol takes no position on what that is.

**Shape of the routine:** the light goes in (2), out through the body (3), out into the futures (5) and the world (7), and back in carrying resources (8–9). The two finger cues (4 and 6) come at the top of an inhale, just before the light moves outward.

## 5. Practice schedule

Conditioning needs repetition. The schedule moves from deliberate practice to cue-triggered use.

| Stage | When the routine is run | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Acquisition** | 3–5 times a day in a calm state, at a fixed time | Learn the sequence; build the agency state |
| **B. Pairing** | Whenever a *mild* negative state is noticed | Pair real triggers with the routine |
| **C. Generalisation** | Across many contexts and trigger types | Make the routine independent of place and trigger |
| **D. Reflex** | Automatically | The trigger starts the routine with little or no deliberate decision |

Progress between stages is judged by the measures in [§8](#8-measurement), not by elapsed time.

## 6. Tiers: multichannel bundles

A **tier** is a named bundle of cues, one per sensory channel, that is activated as a unit. It is the RSOS name for a compound conditioned stimulus.

| Channel | Code | Example (Lion tier) |
|---|---|---|
| Visual | V | Golden lion light; the tier's glyph |
| Auditory | A | The tier's metaphor word, spoken or inner |
| Kinesthetic | K | Warmth in the belly; the fist |
| Olfactory | O | A chosen scent (real or imagined) |
| Gustatory | G | A chosen taste (real or imagined) |

Rules:
- A tier must have at least V, A and K. O and G are optional.
- Each tier carries one **glyph** (visual symbol) and one **metaphor word** (auditory symbol). Glyphs and metaphor words are functional variables: they are the cues being conditioned, not decoration.
- Tiers are numbered. A protocol may use any range (1–10, 1–20, 1–605). Higher tiers are not "stronger"; they are later, more practised bundles.
- A tier is considered **installed** when its measured automaticity passes the reflex criterion in [§8](#8-measurement).

## 7. Configurable programs

The eight-phase override is the reference program. The same runtime runs other programs written in RSX, for example:

- **Interrupt-only:** Phase 1 alone, for very high distress or very little time.
- **Swish:** a fast image replacement (trigger image shrinks, resource image grows), repeated 5–7 times.
- **Counterconditioning drill:** a mild, chosen trigger image paired with a tier bundle, many times.
- **Glyph chain:** a sequence of glyphs, e.g. interrupt → wholeness → future pacing.
- **Mnemonic set:** a principle from a mnemonic set (e.g. Franklin's virtues, Bushidō) surfaced as its glyph in context.

## 8. Measurement

Every run should log the following. The schema is defined in [`rsx/README.md`](../rsx/README.md#session-log).

| Measure | When | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| **Distress (SUD)** | Before and after each run | 0–10 |
| **Agency** | Before and after each run | 0–10 ("I can act now") |
| **Latency** | Pairing stage onward | Seconds from noticing the trigger to "I move, I act" |
| **Automaticity** | Weekly | Self-Report Behavioural Automaticity Index (SRBAI, 4 items; Gardner et al., 2012) |
| **Trigger** | Each run | Free text + channel (V/A/K/O/G/thought/memory) |
| **Optional physiology** | Each run | HRV, EEG, EDA from any device |

**Reflex criterion (proposed, to be validated):** SRBAI mean ≥ 4 of 5 for two consecutive weeks *and* median latency ≤ 3 s across at least 10 real triggers.

## 9. Safety and contraindications

**Hard rules (enforced by the runtime):**
- The practitioner can stop at any moment with one action. Stopping is never an error.
- Haptic amplitude ≤ 40% of device maximum.
- At most 6 device-triggered runs per hour and 20 per day, with at least 90 s between runs (archive range 60–120 s).
- Device-triggered runs require the practitioner's prior opt-in; nothing is triggered silently.
- Scent and taste components require the practitioner to confirm they have no allergy or aversion.

**Do not use as a stand-alone method in:**
- acute crisis, or any risk of harm to self or others: contact local emergency services or a crisis line;
- active psychosis, mania or severe dissociation;
- deliberate processing of traumatic memories, which belongs with a qualified clinician (the protocol may be used *alongside* such treatment with the clinician's agreement).

If distress rises during or after a run, stop, ground (feet on the floor, name five things you can see) and seek support.

## 10. Testable hypotheses

| ID | Hypothesis | Minimal design |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | One run lowers distress and raises agency more than a matched control (paced breathing only) | Within-person randomised, SUD/agency pre-post |
| H2 | Over 4 weeks, latency falls and automaticity rises | Single-case multiple-baseline across participants |
| H3 | Multichannel tiers (V+A+K+O) condition faster than single-channel (V only) | Between-person, matched practice dose |
| H4 | Future pacing improves later behaviour in the paced contexts | Ecological momentary assessment, paced vs unpaced contexts |
| H5 | Device-triggered runs (HRV/EEG) reduce latency more than self-initiated runs | Micro-randomised trial |
| H6 | People, objects and places rehearsed in the futures later evoke more agency on real encounters than matched, unrehearsed ones | Within-person: list rehearsed and comparable unrehearsed elements in advance; rate agency (0–10) on each real encounter by ecological momentary assessment |

H3 has a formal model prediction in [`rsx/rsx/conditioning.py`](../rsx/rsx/conditioning.py). These hypotheses are untested in humans.
