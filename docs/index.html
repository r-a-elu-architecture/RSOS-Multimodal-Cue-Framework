Turn the moment a negative state arrives into the start of an override.

RSOS is an open operating system for changing state on purpose. A short routine of breath, words, gestures, light imagery and sensory cues interrupts the state, redirects it into agency and rehearses it in three future situations. Practised often enough, it is meant to run as a reflex.

The Override Protocol

It starts the moment a negative state appears: a sound, a smell, something seen, a memory. One instant interrupt, then five full breaths, under a minute. Every word, image and gesture is a default you can replace with your own.

  1. InterruptMove, spine straight, head up; clench your fist as you say "I move, I act".
  2. IgniteBreathe in golden lion light to the belly: courage and agency.
  3. RadiateBreathe out as it expands through blood flow, lymph and every cell.
  4. AnchorAt the top of the inhale, touch middle finger to thumb.
  5. Three futuresOne exhale through three futures as your future self: driving, at work, achieving your goal. Everything is connected to you by golden light; hold a gentle smile.
  6. HologramAt the top of the inhale, a golden finger appears before your eyes, pointing up.
  7. ExtendBreathe out the light up, around and into the sky, filling everything that holds power.
  8. ReturnBreathe in as it comes back to the belly carrying every source of agency.
  9. FillBreathe out as it fills every part of you with power.
  10. GatherA simple inhale.
  11. InstallExhale and say "I own": sealed and confirmed.

Four systems, one runtime

RSOS

The cue bundles. Each tier binds a glyph, a spoken metaphor word, a body feeling and, if you choose, a scent and a taste, all activated together.

RSIA

The identity layer: six archetypes (Lion, Eagle, Bear, Leopard, Raven, Tiger) and the values they serve.

RSX

The language programs are written in. It compiles to a timeline any device can play: a browser, a phone, a wearable, a VR scene.

RSSO

Orchestration: triggers, schedules, safety limits and the shared run log.

Write your own program

RSX is a small, tested language with a Python and a JavaScript implementation that are checked against each other. The player has a live editor.

  • Tiers as multichannel bundles: visual, auditory, kinesthetic, olfactory, gustatory.
  • Breath-paced phases, loops over your futures, haptic and tone cues.
  • Safety in the language: vibration stronger than the program's limit will not compile.
  • Runs the archive's RSX v1 vector commands too.
TIER 1 LION {
  V "Golden lion light"
  A "I move, I act"
  K "Warmth in the belly"
}
FUTURES "Driving the car" | "At work"

PHASE interrupt {
  SAY "I move, I act"
  HAPTIC on=300ms off=300ms x=6 amp=0.4
}
PHASE ignite breath=inhale { ACTIVATE LION }
PHASE futures breath=exhale {
  FOR FUTURE { SEE "$future"; WAIT 2s }
}

A model that can be proved wrong

A formal learning model (Rescorla–Wagner for the cue bundles, a habit curve for the reflex) turns the protocol's claims into predictions. They held in all 12 parameter settings tested; none of them has been tested in people yet.

PredictionWhat it means
More channels reach the agency state in fewer runsSupports building tiers from several senses
Each cue alone ends weaker inside a rich bundlePractise and recall the whole bundle, not one cue
A well-practised bundle limits change in the trigger itselfMeasure two outcomes: the reflex, and how the trigger feels

All predictions and design consequences

Where it stands

The specification and software are complete enough to use and test. No human outcome data exists yet: RSOS is published so that researchers, clinics and individuals can run it and share results in one log format. The specification lists six hypotheses with study designs.

It is not a medical device or a treatment. If you are in crisis or at risk of harm, contact local emergency services or a crisis line.
