# Chronology and provenance boundaries

This index retains dates *asserted by historical source records*; it does not certify the dates or factual truth of the claims. Refer to author metadata and file hashes for verification and to source provenance for independent archival evidence.

- **RSOS**: `RSOS_Author_Metadata.txt` declares August 4, 2025; other dated events are embedded in separate records.
- **RSIA**: `RSIA_Author_Metadata.txt` declares August 17, 2025.
- **RSX**: `RSX-Core_Definition.txt` and related documents contain author/version metadata; `BRANCH_B_CLAIMED_COPYRIGHT` is part of the original source folder name, not an independent legal finding.
- **RSSO**: origin and authorship files declare historical lineage; compare exact original content.
- **PASS31B**: later narrative reconstructions and excerpts; underlying messages referenced by `MSG-...` identifiers are **not contained in this ZIP** and must not be treated as independently verified by the ZIP alone.

Modern `architecture/` documents are post-archive indexes. Do not backdate them or treat modern software modules as proof a historical narrative was implemented at the time.
