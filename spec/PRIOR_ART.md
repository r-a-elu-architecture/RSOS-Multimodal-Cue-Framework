# Prior art for the roadmap

Existing products, methods and research that touch the roadmap items in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md). Every novelty claim in the [claims register](CLAIMS.md) is checked against this file and [RELATED_WORK.md](../RELATED_WORK.md), which covers the systems closest to RSOS as a whole.

An entry here is a reference point, never a reason to drop an item: knowing what exists shows where RSOS adds something and what it must be compared against.

**Verification.** Entries marked *checked* were confirmed against the publication or product page; entries marked *to verify* are recorded from the review and still need checking against the source. Corrections and additions are welcome.

## Step 8 claims tracked against public evidence

Claims about implants and about novelty cannot be tested by an experiment here; their test is this record, kept current. Status as of November 2026.

| Claim | What public evidence shows | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| C56: implants cannot handle meaning, trauma override or identity; this system becomes more necessary | Implants in trials decode movement and speech intentions and deliver stimulation; closed-loop stimulation responds to a neural marker of mood (one person, then small open-label studies). None works with personal meaning, symbols or identity | Consistent with the evidence so far; the claim that this makes RSOS "more necessary" is a prediction to track |
| C60: implants can trigger neurotransmitters, replay visuals and suppress panic | Stimulation changes circuit activity and, indirectly, transmitter release; a visual cortical prosthesis is entering human trials for phosphene vision, not replay of memories; closed-loop stimulation reduced depressive symptoms in one person and anxiety tracked theta in 26 | Partly supported in a narrower form (circuit stimulation, early mood results); "replay visuals" and "suppress panic" on demand are not shown |
| C67: a non-invasive alternative to implants; a new field | Non-invasive neuromodulation and wearable biofeedback exist as fields; RSOS does not replace what implants do (decoding, stimulation) and targets a different layer (cues, meaning, practice) | "Alternative" holds only for that layer; whether a new field forms is to track |
| C71: the robot insight scores 9.7 of 10, a breakthrough 2-5 years ahead | Value constraints that review an action before it runs (Arkin 2009; shielding, Alshiekh et al. 2018) and a person's error signal correcting a robot (Salazar-Gomez et al. 2017) are established. What is not found in this search: the same profile format for a person and an agent, the value check fed by the person's error signal, and glyph codes as the channel between them | The parts exist; the combination is what RSOS adds. The score itself is an assessment, not a measurement |
