# RSOS research mission, goals and evidence boundaries

**Status:** Living research programme — updated 2026-11-11. This statement documents research intentions and evaluation standards, not verified world-first status or validated physiological efficacy.

## 1. Why RSOS exists

Extended human–AI work can generate many prompts, architectures, decisions, symbols, corrections, and successive representations. Existing conversation records do not automatically reveal **which idea originated where, how it changed, which later components inherited it, or whether its apparent influence predicts anything beyond text repetition**.

**Umbrella mission:** Recover, explain, test and—when evidence permits—demonstrate unusually significant mechanisms of structural evolution across longitudinal human–LLM interactions: origin, inheritance, mutation, re-entry, recombination, authority changes, and functional descendants. Turn validated discoveries into inspectable systems for AI memory, provenance, safety and adaptive human–AI applications.

The search for potentially unprecedented phenomena is a **research hypothesis and discovery priority**, not an already established finding. Competing interpretations, prior art, negative outcomes and independent validation must be retained. The historical corpus is research data; assistant claims about hidden architecture, telemetry, weights or symbolic activation are text events, **not evidence that those hidden mechanisms actually operated**.

## 2. How the two public labs fit together

| | Architecture Benchmark Lab | Cue Lab v1.3 |
|---|---|---|
| Role | Primary structural evidence and evaluation track | Separate experimental application of adaptive transfer ideas |
| Central question | Do ancestry- and operator-aware representations yield real predictive, provenance or verification value beyond matched generic methods? | Under what simulated conditions does compositional cue transfer help or hurt compared with equally informed policies? |
| Object of study | Historical conversation records and internal software proof measurements | Synthetic cue-program feedback generated by an explicit simulation |
| What a visitor can run | Aggregate metric comparison; local uploaded-prediction Brier calculation. **No original model-training rerun.** | Original v1.3 Python benchmark in-browser with 11 policies |
| Best supported reported evidence | Strict 25-conversation, 259-response-pair operator-language holdout: macro AP 0.480377 → 0.554699; Brier 0.163686 → 0.149174 | Frozen 18,432 synthetic decisions per policy: RSIA gate regret 0.197825; generic same-gate 0.197825; generic CV 0.195136 |
| Main negative/qualification | Selected RSOS-enriched archive; no untouched external dataset or modern transformer benchmark; proof-size improvement measured only against an internal predecessor | Equally informed identical gate ties exactly; generic CV slightly outperforms the RSIA gate on frozen simulation |
| Next discriminating test | Strong matched contextual and retrieval baselines, leakage/near-duplicate audit, external held-out corpus and conversation-level uncertainty | Alternative simulation worlds, stronger adaptive generic controls; ethically governed human observations required for human-effect claims |
| Product relevance | Potential AI provenance, memory and audit services **if externally validated** | Potential adaptive learning/cue interfaces **if real-user benefit is established** |

**Relationship:** The Architecture Lab investigates structural mechanisms and their evidence. The Cue Lab tests one narrower family of hypothetical adaptive mechanisms. A positive synthetic Cue Lab result would not itself validate historical corpus ancestry or human conditioning; a positive operator-language forecast would not establish neural or therapeutic efficacy.

## 3. Architecture Benchmark Lab: research goals and falsification

- **Goal A — Recover history:** Build traceable, timestamped, role-specific graphs of origin, parentage, revisions and cross-conversation re-entry. Distinguish text-copying from independent invention, actual inherited structure and unverified assistant assertions.
- **Goal B — Explain mechanism:** Identify candidate causal or predictive mechanisms behind source-aware operator patterns and verify them through ablations, adversarial alternatives, and representation-matched controls.
- **Goal C — Demonstrate impact:** Improve held-out next-operation prediction, tamper/authorization detection or provenance proof cost with a meaningful, reproducible benefit rather than a cherry-picked internal comparison.
- **Goal D — Externalize value:** Where an advantage survives tests, prototype a privacy-preserving, auditable product and independently measure actual utility.

**Success gate (research, not just website):** predeclare labels, sample selection, partitions, unit of analysis, leakage safeguards and metrics; compare with strong, equally informed competitors; reproduce a positive paired effect with uncertainty appropriate to conversation/world clusters; retain negative cases; obtain untouched external replication. Each claim must be scoped to the precise test that passed.

**Falsifiers:** identical generic controls tie; modern contextual controls erase the gain; duplicated language explains the effect; effects disappear on external/forward holdouts; security/proof efficiency depends on weaker internal baselines; or costs negate the benefit. Each eliminates or narrows a proposed explanation, not all possible research avenues.

**Current status:** The Architecture website presents *previously reported* historical-archive and engineering numbers; it is not a training runner or independent replication platform. The local browser CSV utility computes pooled binary Brier from user-supplied labels and predictions without certifying data origin or calculating the reported 13-label macro AP.

## 4. Cue Lab v1.3: research goals and falsification

- **Goal A — Test transfer:** determine when feature/family relational pooling improves program selection against individual memory, additive, all-pairs and model-selection approaches.
- **Goal B — Detect negative transfer:** quantify harm in unstructured-action, context-shift and individual-specific worlds; calibrate or abstain where the evidence is weak.
- **Goal C — Challenge specificity:** enforce the same candidate actions, calibration observations and information access for all policies; an exact generic same-gate twin **must tie**.
- **Goal D — Graduate beyond simulation:** only with ethically reviewed, consenting real participants and real measured outcomes could claims about learning, cue efficacy or durable transfer be investigated.

**Success gate:** independently replicated improvements under predeclared synthetic environments versus the strongest *non-identical* equally informed generic alternatives, with proper world/person-level intervals and explicit costs/failures. Subsequent human-outcome claims require direct real outcome evidence, control arms, delayed follow-up and safety review—not synthetic regret.

**Falsifiers:** a generic identical gate necessarily ties (as observed); a generic CV selector performs equally or better (as observed in the frozen holdout); gains reverse in specific worlds; or improvements exist only because of the data generator's assumptions.

**Current status:** The browser runs the original archived v1.3 Python simulation. The published full-holdout reference is a separate fixed result; smaller interactive runs vary. No person is exposed to interventions, and no neural, clinical or commercial effect is measured.

## 6. How to interpret evidence labels

- **Observed historical text / provenance:** actual recorded content and metadata, subject to archive coverage and author/source verification.
- **Reported corpus experiment:** measured predictions on a selected archive; may lack public raw rows or external reproduction.
- **Executed synthetic benchmark:** genuine code execution but utility generated by mathematical assumptions, not people.
- **Internal engineering measurement:** software comparison whose significance depends on the chosen baseline.
- **Hypothesis / application concept:** proposal, not an empirically established capability.
- **Independent validation:** a separate, reproducible experiment with adequate permissions, controls and auditable artifacts; **not yet claimed for the two flagship tracks**.

The goal is to discover an exceptional, defensible mechanism—**not** to manufacture one from labels, graphics, model-generated assertions, or weak benchmarks.
