Every claim found in the reviewed design records, recorded as a claim event with the test it becomes. Nothing is discarded and nothing is treated as established: the project is a framework others can program and test, and any claim may prove weaker, true, or stronger than stated. Claims about an individual are written in general form. Generated by `architecture/roadmap.py`.
