Nothing in RSOS starts unchecked

CONTAIN is the safety layer every part of RSOS asks before it acts: the player before a run, the heart-rate trigger before an offer, LINX before an AI proposal changes a plan, the node studio before a plan is approved. It can allow, block or pause, and it always says why. It also watches every program and node over time, and pauses one that stops helping.

What it checks, in the order it checks it, and how things stand in this browser right now:

    Right now

    Report something that felt wrong

    A report linked to a program pauses it until it is looked at. Reports stay in this browser; they are part of a research contribution only if you choose.

    Your node plan

    What the device may do on its own

    Each anchor earns its own autonomy, one stage at a time: manual (you fire it), suggest (the device offers it when it detects stress, you confirm), automatic (the device fires it by itself, within the limits below), and under pressure (also during your pressure practice, only if you choose it). Each stage needs evidence, and when the evidence weakens the anchor drops back by itself.

    Your consent

    Some things work below your awareness or analyse you. None of them happens without a record of your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time.

    Automatic decisions

    WhenWhatDecision
    If you are in crisis or at risk of harm, contact local emergency services or a crisis line now. RSOS is not a treatment and does not replace care.

    The device safety envelope

    Limits no program can exceed, whoever wrote it: the planner, a therapist, LINX or you. A program may set stricter limits for itself, never looser ones. A program outside the envelope does not start (conformance check C14).
