RESEARCH MISSION · TWO DISTINCT LABS

Discover what evolves through human–AI interaction. Test what works.

Our overarching mission is to reconstruct the origin, inheritance, mutation, re-entry and recombination of structures across longitudinal human–LLM conversations, and determine whether those mechanisms produce verifiable value in AI provenance, memory, prediction or adaptive interfaces. Unprecedentedness is a hypothesis to investigate, not a result already established.

Four composable research layers

RSOS · Representation

Personal cue meanings, mnemonic glyphs, metaphors, optional tiers, modalities, contexts and goals.

RSIA · Reliability

Consent, uncertainty, quality checks, evidence watchdogs and justified abstention.

RSX · Programs

Typed operator graphs for mnemonic prompts, voluntary interruption, resource recall, attention, imagery and rehearsal.

RSSO · Orchestration

Individual calibration, feedback history, scheduling, privacy-aware provenance and auditable choices.

What has been demonstrated?

The Architecture Benchmark Lab now foregrounds reported historical conversation results, including a strict forward operator-prediction holdout (macro AP 0.480377 to 0.554699) and internal ancestry-proof comparisons. Those results remain retrospective and require external replication. Synthetic cue transfer is retained as a separate experiment.

Scientific boundary: This project has not established EEG emotion decoding, installed beliefs, durable psychological conditioning, clinical efficacy, or a world-first neurological mechanism. Some equally capable generic controllers matched the RSOS-inspired models exactly.

Applications and research directions

Mnemonic learning

Private visual and audio cues for voluntary recall, including context-specific symbols.

Context-aware AI

Adaptive, user-permitted prompts with uncertainty-aware fallbacks.

VR and accessibility

Programmable cue presentations and choice-based rehearsal.

EEG / BCI research

Interfaces for calibrated signal-quality observations—not emotion inference from raw band power.

Open to independent research

We welcome independent replications, stronger generic baselines, negative findings, formal audits, accessibility studies, and ethics-reviewed neutral human-learning experiments.
