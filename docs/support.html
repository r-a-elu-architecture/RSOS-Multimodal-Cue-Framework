Evidence: this page shows records as written and makes no claim of effect. Not yet tested with people using RSOS.

RSOS is open source and built in the open. Sponsorship funds research and development, first of all the first registered study of RSOS in people: heart-rate sensors, participant payments and a university research partner. Then devices and hosting.

Sponsor on GitHub

Payments go through GitHub Sponsors. GitHub charges no fee on sponsorships from personal accounts. This site takes no payment itself and loads nothing from GitHub.

What support does and does not buy

Your support buys no product, treatment or access. RSOS is a research and development framework, not a medical device. Everything in this repository stays free and open whether or not you sponsor.

Intended use: a research platform, and a tool for qualified practitioners working under their own professional protocol. Not a consumer wellness app. RSOS makes no therapeutic claim; claims about effects in people wait for registered human outcome data.

Funding never changes what is published. Every test is registered before it runs, and every result is published, including null results and failures, in the results register.
