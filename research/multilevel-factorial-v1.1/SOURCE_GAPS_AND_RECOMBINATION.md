## What would establish a real causal link?

Randomize optional module combinations with predeclared consent and supervision on **non-traumatic, low-risk conditioning tasks**. Obtain objective behavioral or psychophysiological response to standardized cues at baseline, immediate post-task, 24 h, 7 d, and a changed context. The first phase should measure feasibility and adverse responses; follow-up tests whether the same cue provokes a changed response. Compare identical exposure time and clinician oversight. Record user-preferred scent and negative reactions; never enforce odor, taste, or bilateral stimulation.

An experiment can test *whether* operators have effects or interactions; it must not claim that tier number equals a clinical recovery stage, or that a participant's beliefs or mental model have been installed. EMDR is an established therapist-delivered protocol; the software's dual-attention label is not equivalent to it.

## Why more modules are not necessarily better

With 7 binary factors there are 128 subsets. Under the strict equal three-module-dose constraint there are 35 eligible triple combinations. Only 7 of the 21 possible pairwise interactions are proposed as source-inspired links here; none is known to have a positive effect on humans. In the synthetic experiment, models can learn when combinations help *because the generator assigns varying effects*. The result does not identify a biologically correct combination.
