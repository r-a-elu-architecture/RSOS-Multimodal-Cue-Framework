State change on purpose, written as a program and measured.

The Override Protocol is a 52-second routine of breath, words, gestures and cues. Here it is a program: compiled with its safety limits, played the same way on a browser, a phone or a wristband, and logged in one format, so researchers and supervised practitioners can test it.

Registered, then measured

Every test is written down, with what would count as a pass and a fail, before it runs. Thresholds are never moved after a result is seen, and failures stay on the record. Every page carries an evidence label, from E1 (the software runs and its tests pass) upward.

Evidence across RSOS: of 15 parts, 6 at E1 Built, 6 at E2 Checked on simulated people, 3 at E4 Tested on recorded data: no positive result yet. Results register: 51 registered tests: 2 pass, 14 mixed, 29 fail, 2 analysis, 4 other. Not yet tested with people using RSOS.

The results register: every test, its result and what supports it. Reproduce them all with one script (REVIEW.md).

The Override Protocol

It starts the moment an unwanted state appears: a sound, a smell, something seen, a memory. One instant interrupt, then five full breaths, under a minute. Every word, image and gesture is a default you can replace with your own.

  1. InterruptMove, spine straight, head up; clench your fist as you say "I move, I act".
  2. IgniteBreathe in golden lion light to the belly: courage and agency.
  3. RadiateBreathe out as it expands through blood flow, lymph and every cell.
  4. AnchorAt the top of the inhale, touch middle finger to thumb.
  5. Three futuresOne exhale through three futures as your future self: driving, at work, achieving your goal. Everything is connected to you by golden light; hold a gentle smile.
  6. HologramAt the top of the inhale, a golden finger appears before your eyes, pointing up.
  7. ExtendBreathe out the light up, around and into the sky, filling everything that holds power.
  8. ReturnBreathe in as it comes back to the belly carrying every source of agency.
  9. FillBreathe out as it fills every part of you with power.
  10. GatherA simple inhale.
  11. InstallExhale and say "I own": sealed and confirmed.

Write your own program

RSX is a small, tested language with a Python and a JavaScript implementation that are checked against each other. The player has a live editor.

  • Tiers as multichannel bundles: visual, auditory, kinesthetic, olfactory, gustatory.
  • Breath-paced phases, loops over your futures, haptic and tone cues.
  • Safety in the language: vibration stronger than the program's limit will not compile.
  • Runs the archive's RSX v1 vector commands too.
TIER 1 LION {
  V "Golden lion light"
  A "I move, I act"
  K "Warmth in the belly"
}
FUTURES "Driving the car" | "At work"

PHASE interrupt {
  SAY "I move, I act"
  HAPTIC on=300ms off=300ms x=6 amp=0.4
}
PHASE ignite breath=inhale { ACTIVATE LION }
PHASE futures breath=exhale {
  FOR FUTURE { SEE "$future"; WAIT 2s }
}

A model that can be proved wrong

A formal learning model (Rescorla–Wagner for the cue bundles, a habit curve for the reflex) turns the protocol's claims into predictions. They held in all 12 parameter settings tested; none has been tested in people yet.

Run it

Python 3.10 or later; RSX itself uses only the standard library. Node.js runs the Python–JavaScript parity tests, and a C++ compiler the firmware tests.

Where it stands

The specification and software are complete enough to use and test. No human outcome data exists yet: RSOS is published so that researchers and qualified practitioners can run it and share results in one log format. The specification lists six hypotheses with study designs.

Intended use: a research platform, and a tool for qualified practitioners working under their own professional protocol. Not a consumer wellness app. RSOS makes no therapeutic claim; claims about effects in people wait for registered human outcome data.

Research and development framework; not a medical device.
