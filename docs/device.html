Your phone, and a wristband if you have one

RSOS starts on your phone: the player, the breath light, the words, the tones and, on Android, the vibration codes on the phone itself. A heart-rate strap can start offers when stress rises. A wristband is optional: it carries the vibration codes on your wrist.

An Android phone is a complete RSOS device on its own. On iPhone, vibration and Bluetooth need a native app, which does not exist yet. Not built on any phone yet: heart rate from the camera, and starting a run from the background with the screen off.

Set up on Android

  1. Open the player in Chrome. r-a-elu-architecture.github.io/RSOS-Multimodal-Cue-Framework/player.html
  2. Install it. Chrome menu (⋮) → Add to Home screen or Install app. RSOS then opens like an app and works offline.
  3. Let it vibrate. In the player, keep Vibrate on under Output. In the phone's settings, Sound and vibration → Vibration and haptics must be on, and Do Not Disturb off while you practise. Test it: on the Glyphs page, choose 🦁 and press Feel it.
  4. Practise once. Press Start, rate how you feel before and after. Every run is kept on the phone only.
  5. Build your own nodes (optional) in the node studio: describe what you want in your own words, approve the plan, and it appears in the player.
  6. Add a heart-rate strap (optional). Turn on Bluetooth and Location (Android asks for Nearby devices). In the player, under Heart-rate trigger, press Connect heart-rate strap, choose your strap, then tick Offer the override when stress is detected. The strap learns your baseline in about six minutes.
  7. Add a wristband (optional) below: Connect by Bluetooth.
  8. Keep a backup. In the player, Save memory stores all your runs in one file you can load on another device.

A wristband

An RSOS wristband plays the codes on your wrist, on its own clock, and stops at once when you press Stop. Connect it by USB (Chrome or Edge on a computer) or by Bluetooth (Chrome on Android).

Not every wristband works. RSOS runs on bands whose software you can replace; most brand watches do not allow that. See which wristbands work. No band yet? The board kit lists the parts (an ESP32 board, a coin vibration motor, a transistor, a battery), the wiring and how to flash it.

Check the band

Seven checks on the band itself, about 15 seconds; the band vibrates during them. The same checks run from a computer with python -m rsso.board --port ….

CheckWhat it requiresResult

Feel a code

Your nodes' vibration codes, and any glyph's code. Strength stays under the safety envelope.

Play a program

The program's vibration (and scent or taste, with consent) plays on the band. Voice and light stay in the player.
