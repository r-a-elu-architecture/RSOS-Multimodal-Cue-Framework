# Publishable anchor — exact scope

**Suggested working title:** Evidence-Gated Compositional Cue Programs: A Programmable, Consent-First Interface for Personalized Symbolic Feedback

**Accurate concise claim:** An executable source-inspired framework represents user-defined glyphs, mnemonic, audio and operator chains as interchangeable modules, with optional tier/state labels and scoped consent checks. Under eight predeclared heterogeneous synthetic response mechanisms, an evidence gate that disables unsupported cue-family transfer reduced heldout decision regret 12.7% relative to an ungated generic all-pairs model on three unseen random seeds (24 seeded world clusters, CI excluding zero). A fresh true context-holdout obtained an 8.46% relative reduction. A generic model with the same relationship features and gate matched exactly. All outcomes are artificial utilities, with no evidence of human neural conditioning.

**Why it is stronger than v1.2:** it tests the mechanism of *when to trust or suspend inferred cue relationships*, including environments where the assumption fails, instead of merely showing that a personalized representation beats a fixed ritual. It preserves consent-first programmable graph compilation and individualized glyph scope.

**Critical novel hypothesis, not established:** A person's meaningful cue relations, applied to a compositional intervention graph with dynamically testable validity and scope, will improve real delayed behavior at matched dose beyond conventional adaptive feedback. This is what an outside lab must test.

**Not claimed:** first invention of model selection; first symbolic conditioning; healing; emotion decoding from EEG; installed neurological levels; superiority over clinical methods; superiority over equally capable generic controllers; unprecedented brain-state transformation.

**External reviewer test:** Run `bash run_all.sh`, inspect the predeclared-world seeds and synthetic effect assumptions, verify generic exact-match ties, attempt to construct a noncompositional world where the gate fails, and then replace artificial utility scores with measured, consented neutral-task outcomes.
