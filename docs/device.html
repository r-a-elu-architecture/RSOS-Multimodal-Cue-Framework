Your wristband

Connect an RSOS wristband, check that it behaves as the device protocol requires, feel your nodes' vibration codes on your wrist and play programs on it. The band plays every program on its own clock and stops at once when you press Stop. Works in Chrome or Edge on a computer (USB), and in Chrome on Android (Bluetooth).

No band yet? The board kit lists the parts (an ESP32 board, a coin vibration motor, a transistor, a battery), the wiring and how to flash it.

Check the band

Seven checks on the band itself, about 15 seconds; the band vibrates during them. The same checks run from a computer with python -m rsso.board --port ….

Feel a code

Your nodes' vibration codes, and any glyph's code. Strength stays under the safety envelope.

Play a program

The program's vibration (and scent or taste, with consent) plays on the band. Voice and light stay in the player.

What the band says

The protocol is RDP/1; this page uses docs/js/rdp.js, whose board check matches rsso/rsso/board.py (tested against the firmware core). Every program is checked by CONTAIN before it is sent.
