# RSOS v2.0.0 — Override Protocol, RSX language, protocol player

This version replaces the v1.0.0 research preview.

## New
- **Override Protocol specification v1.1** (`spec/OVERRIDE_PROTOCOL.md`): the full routine (interrupt, then five breaths: lion light in, through the body, three futures connected by golden light, the hologram finger and the light extended to the world, return, fill, "I own"), practice schedule, measures, safety limits and six testable hypotheses.
- **RSX v2** (`rsx/`): an executable language for multisensory protocols, with matching Python and JavaScript compilers checked against each other, safety limits enforced at compile time, and support for the archive's RSX v1 vector commands.
- **Protocol player** (`docs/player.html`, published on the project site): breath pacing, English voice selection, own-voice recordings, tones, phone vibration, check-in before and after each run, run-log export and a live RSX editor.
- **Learning model** (`rsx/model/`): a Rescorla–Wagner and habit model of the protocol with predictions swept across parameters.
- **Personalised cue engine** (`toolkit/rsos_cue`), reading the player's run log, and the RSIA framework edition (`framework/`).
- **Architecture map** (`architecture/`): what each system and subsystem does and how far it is built, with a separate provenance & rights layer.
- `RELATED_WORK.md`, new README, landing page and licensing (software Apache 2.0; specifications CC BY 4.0).

## Changed
- **Archive public edition:** the fifth RSIA archetype is the Raven (insight and strategic perception); its mnemonic reference is Stephen Covey's *7 Habits of Highly Effective People*; comparison passages with an external system were removed. Fifteen files are affected, with line counts unchanged. Old and new hashes are listed in `archive/provenance/PUBLIC-EDITION-EDITS.csv`. The source ZIP in `archive/original-package/` is the public edition.

## Removed
- The v1.x synthetic benchmark labs (Cue Lab, Cue Lab v1.3, the v1.4 invariance page, the architecture benchmark), the research reports that had no published code or data, and the v1.3 benchmark code.

## Status
No human outcome data exists yet. RSOS is not a medical device or a treatment.
