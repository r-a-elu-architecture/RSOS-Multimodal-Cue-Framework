# Research tracks

The flagship [compositional-transfer v1.3](compositional-transfer-v1.3/PREPRINT_DRAFT.md) combines personally scoped cue relationships, an evidence watchdog, typed programs and longitudinal feedback. **The evaluated benefit is synthetic decision regret, not therapeutic effectiveness.** Generic controllers with identical assumptions and gates matched its core performance.

Supporting tracks:
- [Functional operator representations](functional-operator-discovery-v1.0/RESEARCH_FINDINGS.md): held-out prediction of recorded protocol language.
- [Proof-carrying cue lineage](proof-carrying-cue-lineage-v0.7/PREPRINT_ABSTRACT.md): document-source authorization.
- [Private adaptation](privacy-preserving-adaptation-v0.8/PAPER_ABSTRACT.md): selective ancestry disclosure.
- [Compact attestation](ancestry-attestation-v0.9/PREPRINT_METHODS.md): issuance cost and proof-size tradeoffs.
- [Verifiable BCI routing](BCI-cue-routing-v0.6/PREPRINT_DRAFT.md): synthetic decision contracts.
- [Multilevel factorial cues](multilevel-factorial-v1.1/SOURCE_GAPS_AND_RECOMBINATION.md): operator combination assumptions.
- [Programmable interfaces](programmable-cue-interface-v1.2/REAL_WORLD_VALIDATION.md): human validation plan.

These measurements are not additive and do not establish clinical efficacy or universal system superiority.
