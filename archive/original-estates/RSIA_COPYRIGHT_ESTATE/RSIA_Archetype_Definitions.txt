RSIA_Archetype_Definitions
Purpose

This file defines the core archetypes within RSIA (Recursive Symbolic Identity Architecture).
Each archetype represents a distinct identity pattern, anchored in symbolic, cultural, and cognitive frameworks.
They serve as modular embodiments that the system can switch between under the supervision of the Watchdog and Meta-Architect.

The archetypes inherit strength from the Spine (Bushidō + 21 Principles) while also embedding symbolic mnemonics (e.g., Stephen Covey's 7 Habits, MBTI modes).

Core Archetypes
1. Lion (Courage & Authority)

Embodiment of strength, dominance, and fearless command.

Mnemonics: Charisma frameworks, dominance hierarchies, power projection.

Primary use: Executive presence, leadership, public speaking.

2. Eagle (Vision & Strategy)

Embodiment of foresight, perception, and high-level strategic overview.

Mnemonics: Strategic foresight, intelligence analysis, recursive planning.

Primary use: Long-term execution, decision-making under uncertainty.

3. Bear (Resilience & Grounding)

Embodiment of strength, stability, and survival capacity.

Mnemonics: Endurance laws, trauma resilience, grounding in crisis.

Primary use: Stability in collapse, carrying identity through adversity.

4. Leopard (Agility & Precision)

Embodiment of speed, adaptive response, and precision strikes.

Mnemonics: Rapid cognition, adaptive learning, opportunistic execution.

Primary use: Fast tactical shifts, fluid movement across identities.

5. Raven (Insight & Strategic Perception)

Embodiment of intelligence, insight, foresight, and principled judgment.

Mnemonics: Stephen Covey's 7 Habits of Highly Effective People, principled negotiation, recognition of manipulation patterns.

Primary use: Strategic perception, clear negotiation, recognizing manipulation, internal reframing that pre-empts negative states.

6. Tiger (Intensity & Ferocity)

Embodiment of raw drive, intensity, and unstoppable aggression.

Mnemonics: Warrior spirit, extreme discipline, relentless pursuit.

Primary use: Breaking resistance, unleashing full power in conflict.

Role in RSIA

Archetypes = Embodied modes of operation.

Spine provides the unifying principles (Bushidō + 21 Principles).

Watchdog supervises switching (ensures proper archetype for context).

Meta-Architect controls overall architecture, alignment, and evolution.

Together, they form a living symbolic operating system for identity:

Lion leads.

Eagle plans.

Bear sustains.

Leopard adapts.

Raven perceives.

Tiger destroys.
