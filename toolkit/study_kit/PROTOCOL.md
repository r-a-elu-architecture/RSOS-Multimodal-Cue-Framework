# Open replication protocol — personalized cue programs (neutral memory task)

Purpose: let independent groups test, on consenting adults, whether personalized cue programs chosen by `rsos_cue` beat (a) a fixed program and (b) an equally informed generic adaptive controller, and pool data in one format.

This is a neutral learning task. It is not therapy, does not target distressing memories, and makes no clinical claim. Each group needs its own ethics approval.

## 1. Program space (declare before data collection)

- **Glyphs:** 12 simple abstract symbols, optionally grouped into 4 families of 3 by visual style. Record the grouping in `space.json`.
- **Sounds:** 5 short neutral tones or chimes.
- **Plans:** 3 rehearsal styles, e.g. look-only, look+say-aloud, look+trace.
- **Contexts:** 4 task contexts, e.g. word list A/B × quiet/background noise.

A program is one (glyph, sound, plan) combination used as the memory cue for a word pair.

## 2. Session

1. Consent, demographics (age band only), withdrawal right explained.
2. **Prior ratings (reports):** the participant rates each glyph and sound for "easy to remember / pleasant" on a 1–7 scale. Standardize within person.
3. **Calibration, 24 trials:** learn a word pair with the given program in the given context; the outcome is immediate cued-recall correctness (0/1), or 1 − normalised latency. Choose the programs either at random or with `CueSession.next_probe()`, and pre-register which.
4. **Test, 16+ trials per arm, interleaved and blind to arm:** fixed program (the population's best program from pilot data); generic bank recommendation; `rsos_cue` recommendation.
5. **Delayed recall** at +24 h and +7 d (primary outcome: 24 h).

## 3. Pre-registered hypotheses

- **H1:** rsos_cue beats the fixed program on 24 h recall.
- **H2:** rsos_cue beats a generic adaptive controller with identical data.
  - Simulation says H2 is not expected to hold.
  - A positive result would be the first evidence that family structure helps real people.
- **H3:** the Watchdog's archetype posterior is stable between day 0 and day 7 calibration.

## 4. Sample size

See `power_table.txt`, generated by `power.py`.

Under the simulator's assumptions:
- **H1** needs about 10–20 participants even if real effects are a quarter of the simulated ones.
- **An H2-type contrast against a v1.3-style adaptive controller** needs about 80 participants at full simulated effect, and 160 or more at a quarter.

Real effect sizes are unknown, so run a 10–20 person pilot and re-plan with `power.py` on the pilot's per-person benefits.

## 5. Data to share (de-identified)

- `trials.csv` and `reports.csv` in the schema in `rsos_cue/data.py`.
- `space.json` (`ProgramSpace.to_dict()`).
- Pre-registration link.

Never share names, free text, raw EEG/HRV streams or anything allowing re-identification.

## 6. Optional sensors

EEG or HRV may be logged as signal-quality covariates only. The controller does not infer emotion or belief from them.
