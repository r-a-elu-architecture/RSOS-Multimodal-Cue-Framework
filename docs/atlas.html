The trigger atlas

One feeling reaches you along many paths: a sound, a smell, a place, a face, a thought. For a week or two, note what is present at moments across the day and how your state moves. The atlas fits every path at once and shows which ones really shift you, by how much, and how sure it is. Then work on one node, and measure whether the change travels to the ones you never touched.

Rerouting

When a strong trigger appears, the device can offer your best anchor, or play it by itself where the anchor has earned that on the Safety page. Try it: say which trigger is here.

The descent

A guided trance through your own network, drawn live in three dimensions. You go down toward the strongest node, let light flow into it from the level above, and watch the change travel along every thread. Before and after, you rate one trigger you will not work on: that is the test.

Sit comfortably. When you are ready, start.

Uses WebGL; without it the same scene is drawn in two dimensions. With reduced motion set on your device, the camera moves in gentle cuts. Stop at any moment. If something feels wrong afterwards, use the Safety page.

Did the change spread

Map once, map again with no treatment (the repeat shows how much triggers fade by themselves), work on one node, then map a third time. The change on the nodes you never treated, beyond that fading, is the transfer. The comparison with a timeline session is a trial: network trance or a timeline.

Change in each trigger's weight after treatment, in points, beyond the fading measured by the repeat mapping. Below zero is calmer.

Higher nodes

Instead of a trigger, work from a level above the problem: higher by abstraction (the words, the one who chooses, who you are, what matters, what your life is for) or higher by scope (the room, the city, the country, the world, all living things). After each session, rate how your untreated triggers feel. The sweep shows whether the benefit rises, peaks or falls as the level goes up.

The glyph route

The original glyphs of the tier ledger, in ledger order, as a path to walk in memory. Study the route, then walk it: for each glyph, choose the one that comes next. Each walk is timed, and the page shows how the route settles.
