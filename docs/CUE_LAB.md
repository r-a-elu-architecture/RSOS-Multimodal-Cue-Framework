# RSOS Cue Lab — browser demonstration

Launch: https://r-a-elu-architecture.github.io/RSOS-Multimodal-Cue-Framework/cue-lab.html

This static, client-side educational demonstration lets visitors compose cue programs, inspect an illustrative evidence-gated decision and compare its simulated loss to an always-transfer baseline.

## Evidence boundary
- No personal health, neural or psychological outcome is measured.
- All comparison results are generated by a seeded synthetic model. They are **not** the published v1.3 benchmark.
- The model's latent outcome generator and evidence-gated policy share assumptions; a favorable result does not prove architectural superiority.
- No network request is made by the application JavaScript; external links and badge assets on the parent research site are separate.
- No user accounts or backend services are needed.

## Reproduce
1. Open the demo URL and click **Run synthetic comparison**.
2. Keep seed 42, 500 cases and threshold 70%.
3. Change threshold, sample count and seed to inspect sensitivity.
4. Export JSON and verify the fields `kind: synthetic_illustrative_demo` and `not_published_v13: true`.

## Implementation
Single file `docs/cue-lab.html`, with plain HTML/CSS/JavaScript, deterministic LCG random generator, and no runtime dependencies.
